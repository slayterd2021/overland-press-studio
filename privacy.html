Privacy & data.

A straightforward explanation of this website and our proposed research use.

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This website

This static company-information website has no checkout, signup forms, customer accounts, advertising trackers or third-party analytics in its site code. It does not set cookies or use browser storage. Fonts and illustrations are served as part of the site, without third-party font services.

Hosting information

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses and request information, to operate and secure its services. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for information about its practices. This notice does not claim that hosting infrastructure collects no data.

Proposed research

We are seeking permission for read-only, AI-assisted research into aggregate audience interests and cultural themes to inform original product development. Platform access and analysis will be limited to authorized uses and applicable terms.

We do not intend to sell or distribute Reddit datasets, posts, comments, usernames or user profiles, reproduce users’ content on merchandise, or train or fine-tune models on Reddit data. This describes our proposed use, not an assertion of platform approval.

Future changes

If shopping, contact forms, analytics or other data-processing features are introduced, this information will need to be updated before those features launch. We will keep hosting-specific disclosures current as providers or features change.
