What we are building

Overland Press Studio is an emerging online brand developing original apparel, accessories, home décor and gifts. Our creative directions bring together nature, geometric forms, everyday expression and relatable humor.

Who we create for

Adults looking for expressive everyday objects and thoughtful gifts. Our planned range includes T-shirts, tote bags, mugs, wall art, notebooks and wrapping paper.

How products will reach you

Initial sales are planned through Etsy, using third-party print-on-demand fulfillment. Collections are in development. This site does not accept orders, payments or customer account registrations. Product availability and shipping destinations will be specified in live listings when available.

Research and responsible development

Cultural and market research informs our creative direction. Our process includes artwork preparation, quality review and originality-risk screening before release. These checks reduce risk; they are not a guarantee of legal clearance.
